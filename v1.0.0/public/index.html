How normalized FCF is calculated

01

Operational CapEx

Operating cash flow minus tangible property, plant & equipment purchases and routine capitalized software/intangibles.

02

Economic FCF (post-SBC)

Standard FCF minus Stock-Based Compensation, treating equity issuance as a true operational labor expense.

03

50/50 Margin Blending

Combines simple average annual margin and aggregate volume-weighted margin equally to protect against both small-year skews and scaling shifts.

04

Unlevered EV Multiple

Enterprise Value is compared to Unlevered Normalized FCF (adding back after-tax net interest) to provide an apples-to-apples firm valuation.

Banks and insurers are excluded because conventional FCF is not an appropriate primary valuation method for them.

Near-term solvency is actively verified: companies where short-term debt due within 12 months exceeds available cash plus annual cash flow are disqualified to protect against sudden refinancing cliffs.

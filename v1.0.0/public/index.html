Valuation & Normalization Framework

Transparent accounting methodology designed to eliminate distorted standard cash flow reporting.

01

Comprehensive CapEx

Deducts purchases of physical Property, Plant & Equipment alongside routine capitalized software development and intangible asset acquisitions.

02

Economic (Post-SBC) FCF

Treats Stock-Based Compensation as an ongoing economic labor expense, exposing companies that use cash buybacks to mask dilution.

03

50/50 Margin Normalization

Combines simple arithmetic mean margin and aggregate volume-weighted margin equally across 6 to 10 years, applied to current revenue.

04

Unlevered Enterprise Valuation

Evaluates Enterprise Value against Unlevered Normalized FCF (adding back after-tax net interest) to provide capital-structure neutrality.

Financial Institutions Excluded: Banks and insurers (SIC 6000–6799) are excluded as standard operating cash flow formulas are inapplicable to fractional reserve institutions.
Near-Term Refinancing Test: Companies where short-term debt due within 12 months exceeds cash plus annual cash generation are flagged to protect against solvency shocks.

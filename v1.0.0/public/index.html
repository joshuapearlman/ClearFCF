How Normalized FCF Is Calculated

01

Operational CapEx

Operating cash flow minus physical property, plant & equipment purchases and routine capitalized software/intangibles.

02

Economic (Post-SBC) FCF

Standard FCF minus Stock-Based Compensation, treating equity issuance as a real operational labor expense.

03

50/50 Margin Blending

Combines simple annual average margin and volume-weighted aggregate margin equally across 6 to 10 years, applied to current revenue.

04

Unlevered EV Multiple

Compares Enterprise Value to Unlevered Normalized FCF (adding back after-tax net interest) for capital-structure neutrality.

Banks and insurers are excluded because conventional FCF is not an appropriate primary valuation method for them.

Near-term solvency is actively verified: companies where short-term debt due within 12 months exceeds available cash plus annual cash flow are flagged to protect against sudden refinancing cliffs.
